How SafeJob Works
Four AI modules analyze every job offer in seconds
🤖
NLP Analysis
Our ML model scans the job description for trafficking-specific language patterns trained on 17,000+ real and fake job postings.
🔍
XAI Word Highlights
LIME explainability shows you exactly which words triggered the alert — so you understand why a job is flagged.
💰
Salary Check
We compare the offered salary against Indian market averages using Z-score analysis to detect unrealistic promises.
📍
Location Risk
NCRB crime data maps trafficking hotspots across India. High-risk destination cities raise the overall alert level.
⚖️
Combining Engine
All four scores are weighted and combined into a single Final Risk Score with a clear verdict: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL.
🚩
Reporting Portal
Users can report suspicious jobs directly. Every 30 reports triggers automatic model retraining to stay ahead of new scams.
