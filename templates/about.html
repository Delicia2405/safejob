About SafeJob

A final year project built to protect India's most vulnerable migrant workers from human trafficking traps disguised as job offers.

🎯 Our Mission

Every year, thousands of migrant workers in India fall victim to fake job offers that lead them into human trafficking situations. These offers promise high salaries abroad, free visas, and easy work — but deliver exploitation.

SafeJob was built to give workers a simple tool: paste a job offer, and our AI tells you in seconds whether it's real or a trap. No technical knowledge required. Free. Always available.

We use the same technology that powers large-scale fraud detection systems — NLP, explainable AI, statistical anomaly detection, and crime data — and make it accessible to anyone with a phone.

⚖️ The Combining Formula

Final Score = (0.40 × NLP Score) + (0.20 × Salary Anomaly) + (0.40 × Location Risk)
Scores above 0.75 → CRITICAL  |  0.55–0.75 → HIGH  |  0.35–0.55 → MEDIUM  |  Below 0.35 → LOW
